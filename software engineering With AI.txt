Slide-15-- The Devops Perspective
- SE operates across two intertwined domains:
- 1. User-side operations- deployment, reliability and user feedback

- The goal: reduce uncertainity in both product and process by shortening feedback cycles and increasing observability.
- DevOps makes software engineering a living system of continuous learning and adjustment.
